Chmelnice ⇄ Václavské náměstí, there and back

0 / 8

Everything on this page rides on trams 5 and 9, which run the identical route between Chmelnice and Václavské náměstí — 13 segments, about 5.7 km each way. That coincidence is what makes the trip worth a round: 11 shares the first six stops then genuinely splits off, giving a real wrong-line test on the way out, while 5 and 9 never split at all, giving a real false-positive test on the way back. Cards 3, 4 and 5 are the ones that matter. Card 3 needs the screen on the whole way out; cards 4 and 5 split the return between them, so the long screen-off gap never hides a prompt you were meant to catch. The rest you can do while waiting on a platform. Card 1 is best done at home before you leave.

Before you leave

Is your old progress still on the map?

Every segment id changed this week — they used to be keyed on the station, which meant one id could name three different legs, and riding one lit up all three. On first load the app remaps your saved ids to the new scheme. 1201 map cleanly; 24 covered several legs each and are dropped rather than guessed. Do this at home, before you set off.

  3. Check the km explored figure against what you remember. A drop of a few hundred metres is expected; a drop to near zero is not.

Priority · outbound

Pick the wrong line on purpose — then switch

Trams 11 and 5/9 share the track from Chmelnice all the way to Nákladové nádraží Žižkov, then split: 11 turns off to Mezi Hřbitovy and Želivského, while 5 and 9 carry on to the second Nákladové nádraží Žižkov platform and Olšanská. So a wrong pick should stay silent over the shared part and only complain once the paths genuinely part — that is the whole design.

6shared stops
  1. At Chmelnice, board whichever of 5 or 9 arrives first — but in the app pick 11.
  2. Ride quietly to Nákladové nádraží Žižkov. Nothing should complain over Strážní, Vozovna Žižkov, Ohrada, Biskupcova — this is shared track and a prompt here would be a false positive worth knowing about.
  3. Just after Nákladové nádraží Žižkov, the real tram turns toward Olšanská while line 11 expects Mezi Hřbitovy. The deviation prompt should fire here.
  4. Choose switch to line 5 (or 9, whichever you are actually on) from the options offered.
  5. Then keep watching for two more stops: the line-11 segments you had wrongly collected should go grey again, the correct line should light up, and crediting must carry on at Olšanská and Olšanské náměstí — proving the switch is live, not a one-off.
Careful: the picker at Chmelnice also lists 98. That is the night tram (roughly 22:00–04:30) — do not pick it during the day, it is not a wrong-line test, just a line that is not running.
Confirm: where the prompt fired (which stop you had just passed), whether every plausible line was offered as a switch option, and whether crediting continued afterwards.

Nothing should ever fire. Any prompt on this leg is a false positive, and false positives are worse than misses here.
