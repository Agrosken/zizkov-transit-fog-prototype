Žižkov Transit Fog · Field Test — Round 8

Metro circuit: Želivského → Muzeum → Florenc → Můstek

0 / 5

First metro round — nothing underground has ever been tested. Metro detection zones are built completely differently from surface stops, because GPS does not work down there at all: a 150m circle around the platform, unioned with one circle per cluster of station entrances. Those numbers are a proposal, and this outing is what decides them. Seven stations split into two entrance clusters — Můstek, Florenc, Anděl, Karlovo náměstí, Národní třída, Náměstí Republiky and Kobylisy — the rest get one. The circuit uses all three lines and all three interchanges in scope. Every line is tagged 🚇 real (something you physically do) or 📱 app (something you tap).

Priority

Does a metro stop credit at all with no GPS?

No metro ride has ever been recorded, so this whole mode is untested. Ride metro A from Želivského to Muzeum (Jiřího z Poděbrad and Náměstí Míru in between). Underground there is no signal, so crediting depends entirely on a fix landing in a station's zone as you go down or come up.

3stops
  1. At Želivského, tap Get On before going down, while you still have sky. Pick Želivského and metro A.
  2. Ride to Muzeum. Do not touch the app underground — just note whether the status line says anything at all when you arrive.
  3. At Muzeum, before changing lines, check the map: did any of Jiřího z Poděbrad, Náměstí Míru or Muzeum turn from grey to the line-A green?
  4. If nothing credited, tap Export right there — the fix log will show whether GPS produced anything at all down there, which is the part I cannot guess.
Confirm: how many of the three segments credited, and whether the app stayed usable (no crash, no stuck "Getting your location") with GPS absent for minutes at a time.
Priority

Two-cluster stations — enter one end, leave the other

This is the case you raised. Where a station's entrances are far apart it gets two separate zones rather than one big one; Můstek and Florenc are both split that way. The test is whether coming up at the opposite end from where you went down still registers as the same station.

2stations
  1. At Florenc, note roughly which entrance you use when changing from C to B — the Křižíkova end or the Na Florenci end.
  2. At Můstek, surface deliberately via an exit at the far end from where you entered the platform: Václavské náměstí versus Na Příkopě.
  3. Once outside with a real fix, check whether the app still considers you at Můstek — status line, and whether the Můstek segments credited.
  4. If it did NOT register, note which exit you used. That tells me whether the two clusters need merging into one larger zone after all.
Confirm: whether exiting at the far end still counted as that station, at both Florenc and Můstek.
Priority

Same tunnel both ways credits once, not twice

Both directions of a metro line are deliberately merged into one creditable segment — they share the same tunnel, so riding back should not hand out new progress. Never exercised, because there has never been a metro ride.

–return leg
  1. Note the km-explored figure after you surface at Můstek.
  2. Ride metro A back the other way, retracing the stretch you already covered outbound.
  3. Check the km figure again — the retraced part should add nothing. Only genuinely new stretches should increase it.
Confirm: retracing the same tunnel added no distance, and the map shows no double-counting.
Check

Changing lines at one station

Muzeum (A/C), Florenc (B/C) and Můstek (A/B) are all interchanges. Each change means ending one ride and starting another at the same physical place — worth confirming that does not confuse the pickers or credit the wrong line.

3changes
  1. At each change, tap Get Off and pick the station, then Get On again and pick the new line.
  2. Watch the line picker: at an interchange it should offer both lines serving that station.
  3. Note whether the stop picker finds the station at all while you are underground — with no fresh fix it should let you type the name instead.
Confirm: each change recorded as its own ride on the correct line, and the picker was usable underground.
Check

Where does the dot reappear when you surface?

The 150m platform circle exists to catch a fix taken as signal returns. Whether 150m is right depends on how far from the platform you actually are when the phone reacquires — which only a real ride can say.

–whole outing
  1. Each time you come up, watch how long before the blue dot reappears and roughly where it lands relative to the station.
  2. Note any station where the dot appeared a long way off — a block or more — since that is what would need a bigger radius.
  3. Note the opposite too: anywhere the zone feels far too generous, thinking you are at the station while you are clearly streets away.
Confirm: a rough sense of reacquisition distance per station. This is what sets the radius, so approximate is fine.
